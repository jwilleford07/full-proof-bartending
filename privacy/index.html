Privacy Policy

Your event details stay handled.

Full Proof Bartending collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, coordinate bookings, plan event service, and improve follow-up.

Information we collect

The basics needed to plan a bar.

When you submit an inquiry, Full Proof Bartending may collect your name, email address, phone number, event date, event location or venue, guest count, event type, service window, menu preferences, package selections, add-on interest, referral source, and notes you choose to provide.

If you pay a date-hold deposit or use a third-party checkout provider, payment details are processed by the payment provider. Full Proof does not need to store full card numbers on this website.

Use

How we use information.

We use inquiry and booking information to respond to your event request, check availability, recommend a package, coordinate event logistics, prepare service, request feedback after completed events, and maintain internal business records.

Sharing

When information is shared.

We may share limited details with service providers that help operate the business, such as website hosting, CRM, email, payment processing, and event operations tools. We do not sell personal information.

Text messaging

SMS stays tied to your event.

If you opt in to receive text messages from Full Proof Bartending, we may use your phone number to respond to your event inquiry, schedule consultations, send quote or date-hold reminders, coordinate event details, and request post-event feedback. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
