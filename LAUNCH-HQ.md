## Core Positioning

Full Proof Bartending is a luxury mobile bartending service for private events across LA, Long Beach, and OC. The brand should sell confidence: a complete, polished bar experience that looks premium, runs cleanly, and lets hosts feel fully handled.

## Differentiators

- Professional self-contained craft bar setup.
- Power, running water, rinser, LED lighting, organized tools, and ice press capability.
- Full Proof Pro Bar and Full Proof Elite Bar rental paths that protect presentation and workflow.
- Clear ice expertise as proof; premium ice upgrades are scoped with Full Proof Pro Bar or Elite.
- Premium add-ons including smoke, espresso martinis, custom infusions, organic dehydrated citrus, premium garnish styling, and menu display/signage.
- Founder expertise from building a clear ice program from zero into real Los Angeles bar and restaurant accounts.
- Southern hospitality with Southern California polish.

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with mobile bartending services, not branding or ice history.
- Treat clear ice as the signature detail, not the whole identity.
- Use the setup as proof of professionalism and premium value.
- Keep the tone confident, plain, warm, and elevated.
- Avoid gimmicky luxury language. The brand should feel expensive because it is specific, capable, and restrained.

## Recommended Homepage Flow

1. Luxury mobile bartending for private events.
2. Service promise: cocktails, clear ice, complete bar setup.
3. Professional setup section.
4. Packages or event types.
5. Signature details and ice add-ons.
6. Founder credibility and hospitality story.
7. Gallery and press/trust signals.
8. Simple booking CTA.

## Agent Roles

### Launch Manager

Owns the full launch plan, priorities, deadlines, and handoffs. Keeps the brand practical, premium, and revenue-focused.

### Website Conversion

Improves page flow, copy, CTAs, trust signals, gallery strategy, and inquiry conversion.

### Offer and Positioning

Defines packages, add-ons, pricing logic, differentiators, and high-converting sales language.

### Operations and Event Systems

Builds checklists, pack lists, client intake, setup workflow, strike workflow, and quality standards.

### Local Growth and Partnerships

Owns outreach to planners, venues, caterers, private chefs, event rental companies, photographers, florists, and local hosts.

### Content and Social Proof

Turns events, setup details, ice craft, and founder story into simple content that supports booking.
