Common Questions

The questions worth asking before you book.

Do you provide the alcohol?

Full Proof is dry-hire, meaning the host purchases the alcohol. That avoids catering markups and gives you control over the bottles. If you want the easier path, Full Proof can guide a Total Wine order, map quantities to the menu, coordinate ice, and keep the process organized.

Do you check IDs and refuse service when needed?

Yes. Full Proof is built for responsible adult events. Guests who appear under 21 should have valid ID. Service may be refused or paused for underage guests, visibly intoxicated guests, unsafe setups, or anything that puts guests or the bar at risk. The host remains responsible for purchasing alcohol legally and keeping the event environment safe.

What do you need from the venue?

A clean place to load in and enough space for the selected bar footprint. With Pro or Elite, Full Proof can bring power planning, fresh and gray water, running water, rinser, utility sink, ice storage, prep surfaces, service mats, glass drying mats, and organized tools. We do not need a venue bar, house sink, or borrowed folding table for the full setup.

What happens to leftover ingredients?

Full Proof tries to minimize waste before and after service. When the ingredients and timing make sense, Joe can turn remaining juices, syrups, fruit, and compatible cocktail ingredients into large-format batches before leaving.

How much ice do you bring?

Precision-cut clear ice is included for Full Proof cocktails. Larger clear-ice upgrades require Pro or Elite so handling, storage, service pace, and presentation stay clean. Events up to 40 guests can add Pro for $100. Events from 41-125 guests require Elite for $200. Events at 126+ guests require manual review.

Are smoked cocktails and smoke bubbles included?

They are instant-book upgrades when they fit the menu and guest flow, and they require Pro or Elite. Smoke service is +$100. Smoke plus bubbles is +$150. Full Proof can bring smoke, spice, cloche-style presentation, and smoke-bubble capability when the setup supports it.

How do espresso martinis price out?

Espresso martini service is +$100 and requires Pro or Elite. Hand-pulled espresso is best for smaller VIP rounds. Batched espresso is better when the line needs to move.

What is the booking process?

Start by sharing the event basics. Choose base bartending, Pro Bar, or Elite Bar, then continue to the secure 50% date hold if the event fits instant-book rules. Menu direction and shopping details can be finalized after the date is held. If the event needs more detail, Joe will recommend the right custom quote.

Can I reserve before choosing cocktails?

Yes. The date, service window, guest count, and event type matter first. Menu direction, shopping list, signature cocktails, and premium ice details can come after the date is safe.

How much is the deposit?

Instant-book packages use a 50% date-hold deposit. Base bartending is $599 for 3 hours or $699 for 4 hours. Pro adds $100. Elite adds $200 and is required for 41-125 guests. Larger weddings, brand events, 126+ guest events, and custom quotes may require a different deposit because staffing, prep, and logistics are more involved.
