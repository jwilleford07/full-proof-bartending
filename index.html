Common Questions

Details for hosts.

Do you provide the alcohol?

Full Proof operates as a dry-hire service, meaning the host provides and purchases the alcohol. This avoids retail markups and gives you full control over the selection. If you want a hands-off experience, liquor and ice concierge service is available: Full Proof can guide a Total Wine order, map quantities to the menu, coordinate clear ice and standard ice needs, and keep the process simple so you can be as involved as you want to be.

What do you need from the venue?

A clean place to load in and enough space for the bar footprint. Full Proof brings the professional mobile bar infrastructure: generator-capable power, fresh and gray water, a 110v pump, running water, integrated Decent Espresso rinser, custom utility sink, custom portable stainless steel ice bin, prep surfaces, service mats, glass drying mats, and organized tools. We do not need the venue to provide a bar, sink, running water, or house power for the full setup.

What happens to leftover ingredients?

Full Proof tries to minimize waste before and after service. When the ingredients and timing make sense, Joe can turn remaining juices, syrups, fruit, and compatible cocktail ingredients into large-format batches before leaving, so you can keep pouring, save them for later, or give them as gifts.

How much ice do you bring?

Every Full Proof package includes hand-carved clear ice cubes for the cocktails we serve. If your event requires extra ice volume, specialty pressed shapes, or standard ice for chilling beer and wine, we can scope that into your custom quote.

What is the booking process?

Start by sharing the event basics. If the Launch Special fits, choose Basic at $699 or Cups + Garnishes at $799, then continue to the 50% date-hold deposit. Menu direction and shopping details can be finalized after the date is held. If the event needs more detail, Joe will recommend the right custom quote.

Can I reserve before choosing cocktails?

Yes. The date, service window, guest count, and event type matter first. Menu direction, shopping list, signature cocktails, and premium ice details can be finalized after the date is held.

How much is the deposit?

The Launch Special uses a 50% date-hold deposit: $349.50 for Basic or $399.50 for Cups + Garnishes. For the first 10 eligible bookings, promo code FOUNDER makes Basic $599 with a $299.50 deposit and locks the $699 base rate for similar future parties. Larger weddings, brand events, and custom quotes may require a different deposit because staffing, prep, and logistics are more involved.
