The clear-ice advantage

Signature clear ice is included because details change the drink.

Full Proof is a mobile bartending service first: polished hosting, thoughtful cocktails, smooth service, and a bar that looks composed from the first pour to the final cleanup.

Clear ice is the differentiator. Before Full Proof, founder Joe Willeford started a clear-ice program from zero: no prior ice experience, no inherited playbook, just a need he saw before most bartenders were paying attention. He consulted with Camper English before starting the business, learned to operate a Clinebell ice maker, slab 300-pound blocks with a chainsaw, rebuild a Biro meat saw for ice cutting, and market the finished product to bars and restaurants across Los Angeles County. That operation later became Cold Standard Ice and continues today as Clear Standard Ice.

Press Quoted by Camper English in Distiller Magazine’s “How to Start a Big Clear Ice Program” on Clinebell equipment and large-format clear ice production.
Network The work connects Full Proof to the serious clear-ice world, including Camper English’s directional-freezing research and Jimmy Yeager’s professional ice production lineage.
