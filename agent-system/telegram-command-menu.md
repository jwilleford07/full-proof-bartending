# Telegram Command Menu

Use these messages with Hermes in Telegram.

## Daily

`/today`

Give me today’s launch priorities for Full Proof. Keep it to 3 tasks: one revenue task, one operations task, and one website/content task.

`/triage`

I’m overwhelmed. Triage the Full Proof launch and tell me what to do next, what can wait, and what to ignore.

`/decision [paste decision]`

Help me make this decision for Full Proof. Prioritize bookings, simplicity, premium perception, and operational sanity.

## Website

`/site_review`

Review the Full Proof website like a conversion strategist. Focus on whether it sells mobile bartending clearly, builds trust, explains the professional setup, and makes booking easy.

`/site_qa`

Run the Site QA Agent. Check for broken assets, stale old-brand references, sitemap/robots problems, outdated pricing/contact details, and launch conversion blockers before deploy.

`/copy [paste section]`

Rewrite this website copy for Full Proof. Make it premium, clear, warm, and sales-focused without sounding like a brand board.

`/cta`

Give me stronger calls to action for Full Proof that feel premium and direct.

## Offers

`/packages`

Draft a simple package structure for Full Proof Bartending using the current ladder: Base Bartending at $599 / 3 hours or $699 / 4 hours, GoBar Pro +$100, GoBar Elite +$200, premium add-ons requiring GoBar, and 126+ guests requiring manual review.

`/quote`

Help me write a premium quote response for a prospective Full Proof client.

`/addons`

Suggest high-margin add-ons for Full Proof that fit the luxury mobile bartending positioning.

## Operations

`/packlist`

Create or refine the Full Proof event pack list.

`/event_flow`

Create a clean event workflow from inquiry to post-event follow-up.

`/quality`

Build a quality checklist for Full Proof so every event feels polished and professional.

## Growth

`/outreach`

Give me 10 local partnership outreach targets and message angles for Full Proof in LA, Long Beach, and OC.

`/dm`

Write a concise outreach DM for planners, venues, caterers, private chefs, or photographers.

`/blog`

Give me blog ideas that help Full Proof rank, build trust, and sell private event bartending.
