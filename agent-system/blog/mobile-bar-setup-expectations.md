## Bartender-only versus a professional mobile bar service

**Bartender-only arrangements** typically assume you supply equipment, ice, mixers, garnish infrastructure, and sometimes glassware. The hire focuses on execution skill behind whatever exists—not necessarily on transforming your patio into an orderly bar.

**Professional mobile bar service** brings the operational spine:

- Ice handling appropriate to drink style and duration  
- Garnish preparation windows that stay neat during peaks  
- Tools and hygiene rhythm consistent with indoor or outdoor conditions

## Typical event arcs (and where bar service matters most)

Most weddings and private parties move through recognizable beats. Bar planning maps cleanly onto them:

**Guest arrival / cocktail portion.** Concentrated demand in a compressed window. Throughput and garnish readiness matter more than bottle flair.

**Transition moments.** Introductions, speeches, or champagne pours benefit from coordination—not improvisation.

**Peak dancing / informal mingling.** Lines shorten when layouts anticipate movement rather than reacting to it.

**Last call and closure.** Quiet clarity prevents rushed pours or uneven cutoff messaging across rooms.

Professional teams communicate these arcs with planners or designated hosts so timing stays humane rather than frantic.

## Space and access: a practical checklist

You do not need a ballroom. You do need honesty about constraints—without catastrophizing small venues.

**Layout.** Approximate dimensions for bar footprint; proximity to electrical access if refrigeration or lighting upgrades matter.

**Ground surfaces.** Level patios, turf, or decking influence stability and spill management.

**Water access.** Useful for rinsing rhythm and hygiene; absence of plumbing nearby simply shapes prep differently—not necessarily a blocker.

**Power.** Dedicated circuits reduce blown breakers during peak blending if your menu trends cocktail-forward.

**Load-in paths.** Elevators, stairs, or narrow gates affect arrival timing—especially for coastal venues or hillside homes common across LA and OC.

Share photos or rough sketches during quoting so staffing and gear recommendations stay grounded.
