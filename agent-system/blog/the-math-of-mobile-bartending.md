# The Math of Mobile Bartending: Why "Dry Hire" is the Best Kept Secret in Event Planning

When you are planning a private event, the bar is usually one of the first things you think about. It’s the gathering point. It sets the tone. 

But when you start getting quotes from mobile bartending services, you might experience a moment of sticker shock. You see a flat service fee—say, $599 or $699—and then you realize: *"Wait, I have to provide the alcohol on top of this?"*

At first glance, a flat rate plus a trip to Total Wine feels like a big number. But when you break down the actual math of how "dry hire" bartending works, you realize it is the single most cost-effective way to host a premium event—while actually getting a better experience.

Here is the real math behind private event bartending in Los Angeles and Orange County.

---

## The Venue Math (Paying Per Drink)

If you host an event at a restaurant, hotel, or traditional venue, you are paying for alcohol at a massive markup. 

A standard craft cocktail in LA or OC runs between $16 and $22. Once you add venue service charges (often 20-22%) and sales tax, you are easily paying **$20 to $25 per drink**.

Let's run a realistic scenario for an intimate 30-person party lasting 4 hours:
- **Guest count:** 30
- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Consumption:** ~4 drinks per person (standard for 4 hours)
- **Total drinks poured:** 120

At a venue charging $16 per craft cocktail:
- Base drink cost: 120 × $16 = $1,920
- Add standard 20% service charge = $384
- Add ~9% LA/OC sales tax = $207
- **Total Venue Bar Tab:** **$2,511**

You are paying $2,500 just for the drinks. And you are drinking from their menu, on their timeline, often waiting in a line at a crowded bar.

---

## The Dry Hire Math (Full Proof Bartending)

"Dry hire" means you hire the professional service, the bar setup, and the expertise, but you purchase the alcohol directly at retail cost. Because we don't hold a retail liquor license, we cannot legally sell you alcohol at a markup. 

**This is your biggest advantage as a host.**

Let's run the exact same 30-person, 4-hour party scenario with Full Proof Bartending:

1. **The Service Fee:** $699 (for a 4-hour Standard Service)
   *This includes the bartender, the bar tools, premium compostable drinkware, menu planning, and our signature hand-carved clear ice.*

2. **The Alcohol (Retail Cost):** ~$350 to $400
   *If you are serving 120 drinks, you need about 7-8 bottles of spirits. At Total Wine or Costco, decent mid-tier to premium spirits (Tito's, Maker's Mark, Espolòn) run $25-$40 a bottle. Add in mixers, juices, and a wine/beer buffer, and a $400 budget is actually very generous.*

**Your Total Cost: ~$1,099**

By paying the flat fee and buying your alcohol at retail, you just saved **over $1,400** on a 30-person party. And if you buy from places like Total Wine or Costco, you can usually return unopened, unchilled bottles the next day.
