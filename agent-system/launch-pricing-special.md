# Launch Pricing Special - Founding Host Offer

Updated: 2026-05-15

## Goal

Use a compelling, below-market launch offer for the first 10-15 bookable events without positioning Full Proof as the cheap option. The offer should create fast conversion, generate reviews, capture photo/video proof, and let Joe overdeliver while the public portfolio catches up to the real product quality.

## Founder Framing

The offer should feel like an opportunity, not a clearance price.

Core message:

> You are getting professional-grade hospitality at an accessible launch rate while a lifelong hospitality professional, bartender, clear-ice maker, and social work student turns years of skill into a mobile bar service unlike anything else in the LA / Long Beach / OC corridor.

This matters because Full Proof is not just selling drinks. It is selling the feeling that the host is cared for, the room is handled, the bar is beautiful, and guests can immediately sense that someone experienced is paying attention.

The social work student detail should be used carefully and sincerely. It should not read like a sympathy discount. It should support the larger story: hospitality, service, care, emotional intelligence, and the ability to make people feel safe, seen, and celebrated.

Founder story:

Joe is proud of Southern hospitality and believes event bartending should feel more cared-for than it often does. The gap he sees in mobile bartending is part of a larger gap he has seen across bars, restaurants, events, and cocktail programs throughout different areas of hospitality in the United States. Too many programs treat "craft cocktails" like another piece of equipment that can simply be added to the operation. In reality, making craft cocktails requires experience, deliberation, prep, technique, and execution.

Generic chain restaurants and overextended hospitality programs often try to accomplish feats they simply are not built to execute well. The result is a wide gap between what is promised and what is actually served, especially when "craft cocktails" are treated as branding instead of technique, ingredients, pacing, prep, and taste.

Many of these programs are in markets that were far behind cultural cocktail centers like New York City and Los Angeles, so they try to hyper-speed craft cocktail development to catch up. The problem is that they skip the learning that happens in the middle: repetition, apprenticeship, palate development, menu discipline, mise en place, service pacing, and the humility to make foundational drinks correctly before chasing the next riff.

COVID changed hospitality in ways the industry is still absorbing. Some of the old standards have not fully returned, and guests can feel the difference. Full Proof is Joe's answer to that gap: a direct, founder-led event bar where the person planning the menu, preparing the ice, building the syrups, setting up the bar, and serving the room is accountable for the whole experience.

The point is not to chase novelty for its own sake. Many guests have never had a properly made Paper Plane, Oaxacan Old Fashioned, Last Word, Sidecar, Pornstar Martini, Boulevardier, or other modern/classic cocktail with quality ingredients and attentive professional execution. Full Proof should position familiar cocktails done beautifully as just as exciting as original riffs.

Owning the business gives Joe the creative control he rarely had while working inside other people's bar programs: ice press moments, smoked cocktails, cocktail films, smoke bubbles, thoughtful NA riffs, clear ice, and menus that are built for the event rather than diluted by too many competing hands. The promise is simple: no intermediaries, no stress. Full Proof takes care of the bar so the host can enjoy the party.

## Market Read

Recent local pricing checks show three bands:

- **Service-only / light-service bartending:** roughly $375-$550 for small events, often with host-provided alcohol, standard call-drink supplies, ice, cups, and basic bar supplies.
- **More complete mobile bartending:** roughly $650-$900+ for small-to-mid-size events once standard call-drink supplies, ice, cups, garnishes, menus, setup, custom syrups, modifiers, or signature cocktails are included.
- **Premium mobile bar / wedding / activation service:** commonly $1,250-$2,400+ once multiple bartenders, portable bars, custom menus, glassware, ice, and production complexity enter the quote.

The opportunity: a $699 launch base can undercut many full-service competitors while still feeling premium if the offer is framed as a founder's portfolio rate and keeps scope clean. The $799 Cups + Garnishes path removes small sourcing friction for hosts without forcing that cost into Basic.

## Launch Upgrade Included

For the first 10-15 events, include one visible premium touch that helps generate photos and reviews:

- **Default included upgrade:** clear ice cubes for cocktails served by Full Proof.
- **If the event is a great portfolio fit:** add one small smoked cocktail or bubble theater moment for up to 20-25 drinks at no charge, only when venue rules, timing, and menu flow make it easy.
- **If the event is not operationally right for smoke/bubbles:** include a small premium garnish upgrade instead.

Do not promise smoke/bubble theater as a universal free inclusion. Use it as a surprise-and-delight lever or written bonus only when the job fits.

- **Smoke bubbles:** +$50 per event when venue rules and drink style allow.

- **Additional custom syrups and modifiers:** quoted additionally for expanded syrup programs, specialty bitters beyond the standard set, oleo saccharum, shrubs, clarified elements, espresso, ginger, tea, spice, foam, or other nonstandard cocktail ingredients beyond the agreed launch menu.

Dehydrated fruit positioning:

> We use premium dehydrated fruit when it fits the cocktail theme because it looks polished, travels cleanly, and helps save you from dumped buckets of unused fresh citrus at the end of the night.

## Mocktail Philosophy

Mocktails are often treated like an afterthought. Full Proof should position NA drinks as part of the same craft system as the cocktail menu: same ingredients, same steps, same processes, and the same care in the glass.

When appropriate, signature mocktails can be built as direct riffs on the event's signature cocktails using NA spirits, shared syrups, fresh citrus, modifiers, garnish logic, and matching presentation. This keeps non-drinkers included in the experience rather than handed a lesser version of the menu.

## Customization Promise

The standard syrup, bitters, garnish, and bar-supply set is the baseline, not a rigid menu. Full Proof can adjust the build around guest needs, event style, NA preferences, allergies, dietary restrictions, spice tolerance, sweetness level, venue rules, seasonality, and the kind of drinks the host actually wants to serve.

Use this language:

> The included bar kit gives us a strong craft baseline, but the menu is not one-size-fits-all. We can tailor syrups, modifiers, garnishes, NA options, spice level, sweetness, and service flow around your guests and the event.

## Menu Efficiency Positioning

Full Proof should position menu planning as a way to make the host's purchase smarter, not just more creative.

Approved client-facing concept:

> If desired, we can optimize your menu around overlapping ingredients, base spirits, and themes so your bar feels abundant without asking you to buy bottles or modifiers you will never use again.

Example:

If Cointreau is part of the bar, the menu can branch into Sidecars, Margaritas, Cosmopolitans, and White Ladies with the right supporting bottles and citrus: lemon, lime, cranberry, brandy, tequila, and vodka. Add ginger beer and the same bar can support mule variations. The point is not to make the menu feel smaller. The point is to make the purchase smarter, reduce wasted inventory, and create drink combinations that feel intentional.

Future site/app idea:

- Build an in-site menu builder once standard cocktails are indexed.
- Let hosts choose a few favorite drinks, spirits, or flavors.
- Show overlapping ingredients and suggest efficient cocktail combinations.
- Flag expensive one-off ingredients that only support one drink.
- Recommend NA riffs using the same syrups, citrus, modifiers, and garnish logic.
- Connect the final menu to a shopping and ice concierge checklist.

## What Not To Include At $699

Protect the economics and the brand:

- Do not include unlimited custom cocktail R&D.
- Do not include alcohol, expanded syrup programs, specialty modifiers, nonstandard cocktail ingredients, or grocery purchasing unless charged separately.
- Do not include spheres, diamonds, branded cubes, or specialty clear ice volume.
- Do not include the full custom bar rental by default; quote it as a $150 add-on unless the booking structure explicitly includes it.
- Do not include weddings, large corporate events, high-volume service, or complex venue load-ins in the $699 base.
- Do not include glassware rentals.

## Launch Review Ask

Every Founding Host booking should trade the enhanced value for proof:

- Ask for permission to capture short photo/video clips of the bar, ice, and drinks.
- Ask for a Google review within 24 hours after the event.
- Ask for one sentence about what made the service feel easy or elevated.
- Ask if Full Proof can tag the host or venue when appropriate.

Suggested post-event text:

> Thank you again for having Full Proof. Since these first events are helping build the public portfolio, a quick Google review would help a lot. One sentence about the bar setup, drinks, or how easy the hosting felt is perfect.

## Offer Expiration

Use a hard internal cap, not a loud public countdown:

- First 10 events: $699 is available broadly for good-fit events, with promo code `FOUNDER` making Basic $599.
- Events 11-15: use selectively for strong portfolio value, weekday dates, great venues, or easy logistics.
- After 15 events: retire $699 publicly and only use it as a private save/relationship rate when strategically useful.

## Decision

Adopt the $699 Founding Host Special as an intro offer, not the permanent base package. The offer is strong because it beats local full-service value, includes visible premium proof through clear ice, includes the flavor program that makes Full Proof cocktails stand out, and gives clients a simple reason to book now without making Full Proof look discounted.
