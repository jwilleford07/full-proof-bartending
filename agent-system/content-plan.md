# Content Plan: Full Proof Bartending

**Purpose:** Practical, repeatable guidance for Instagram, Google Business Profile (GBP), blog, and on-event proof capture—aligned with `agents/content-lead.md` pillars and `hermes-handoff-prompt.md` positioning.

**Tone guardrails:** Premium, calm, capable. Warm hospitality without corniness. Stress **real service details** (mobile bar, ice, garnish, flow of service)—not generic cocktail-influencer fluff. Avoid hype words (*ultimate, insane, viral, luxury overload, hurry, deal, discount*). Prefer plain confidence: what we bring, how it works, where we serve.

**Conversion anchors:** Website `https://fullproofbartending.com/` · Reserve/date-hold CTA · `(949) 771-7148` · `joe@fullproofbartending.com` · Instant-book framing: approachable base pricing, Full Proof Pro Bar/Elite upgrades, and premium add-ons gated behind the right bar footprint.

**Geography reminders:** Long Beach base · LA · OC · Southern California private events.

## 2. Instagram: post concepts (copy-ready outlines)

Each row is **one post**. Swap in real details after events when you have permission.

### Feed carousel / single-image concepts

1. **Hero setup** — Image: wide shot of mobile bar in venue. Caption focuses on self-contained operation and host peace of mind. CTA: quote link in bio / DM “event date + neighborhood.”

2. **Ice detail** — Close-up clear cube + cocktail. One sentence on clarity and slower melt; tie to drink quality without chemistry lecture.

3. **Tools & mise** — Organized rail, jiggers, citrus. Caption: preparation visible to guests = predictable pacing.

4. **Shaker mid-service** — Action shot; caption about rhythm of service (short waits, full glasses).

5. **Garnish discipline** — Peel or herb detail; caption on restraint and freshness.

6. **NA alongside booze** — Two glasses; caption on inclusive menus without treating NA as afterthought.

7. **Corporate calm** — Neutral backdrop; caption: discreet bar flow for mixed-age crowds.

8. **Wedding timeline** — Caption: champagne toast timing + bar open coordination with planner/coordinator.

9. **Outdoor / rooftop context** — Weather/lighting logistics stated plainly (weights, placement, wind)—shows competence.

10. **Behind bar portrait** — Founder/lead; one line on experience + commitment to composed service.

### Reels ideas (15–45s)

- Walk-around of bar closed → open for service (10s) + voice or on-screen text: three things hosts don’t have to worry about.
- Pour + clear ice landing in glass (slow clip) + text: “Same drink. Better control.” (Not “best ever.”)
- 20s “what’s on the cart” pan: water, glassware, garnish, trash—operations as premium.
- Stitch/duet **only** if on-brand; otherwise avoid trend-chasing.

### Story prompts (quick)

- Poll: “Bar inside or terrace?” (engagement + qualification).
- Question sticker: “What’s your event month?”
- Before/after setup (same day)—proof without overselling.

## 5. Proof-capture prompts (on-event checklist)

Use at **every** booked event where photography is allowed. Goal: trustworthy portfolio + Stories without disrupting guests.

### Before guests arrive

- [ ] Wide shot: bar position relative to room (establish professionalism).
- [ ] Closed cabinet / organized back bar—labels tasteful or obscured if rights unclear.
- [ ] Ice detail: cubes in tray or in glass **without** cluttered background.

### During service

- [ ] Mid-pour or shake—face optional; hands + tools acceptable if privacy-sensitive.
- [ ] Guest hands/glasses **only** with consent; prefer anonymous foreground blur.
- [ ] Garnish macro + finished drinks on rail.

### Ops honesty (differentiator content)

- [ ] Water/rinser or ice well in use—shows real infrastructure.
- [ ] Trash/recycle station under bar or discreet side—shows cleanup discipline.

### After (2 minutes)

- [ ] One “team wrap” or locked bar photo if brand-appropriate.

**Note:** No venue or client identifiers in captions without written OK. When in doubt, “private event, Southern California.”
