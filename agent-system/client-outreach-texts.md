# Client Outreach Texts

These text messages are designed to be warm, grounded, and professional—adhering to the brand's guest-first hospitality voice. They avoid sounding overly salesy while effectively sharing the new Full Proof Bartending brand and making a low-friction ask for a testimonial, photos, or referrals.

## Option 1: Direct & Warm (Focus on Testimonial/Photos)

**To Carrie:**
"Hi Carrie! Hope you’ve been doing well. I’m reaching out because I just officially launched the new website for my mobile bartending business, Full Proof Bartending. I'd love for you to check it out when you have a second: [Link] 

Since bartending your event was such a great experience, I was wondering if you might have any photos of the bar/drinks from that day, or if you'd be open to writing a quick sentence or two I could use as a testimonial? No pressure at all, but it would mean a lot as I get this off the ground. Hope you're having a great week!"

**To Wendy:**
"Hi Wendy! Hope everything is going great with you. I wanted to share a quick update—I just launched the new brand and website for my mobile bartending service, Full Proof Bartending: [Link]

I really enjoyed working your event, and as I'm building out the site, I'm looking to feature some of my favorite past clients. If you happen to have any photos of the drinks or the bar setup from your party, or if you'd be willing to share a quick testimonial about the service, I would be incredibly grateful. Either way, hope you're doing well!"

---

## Option 2: Conversational (Focus on Referrals & Catching Up)

**To Carrie:**
"Hey Carrie, Joe here! Hope you're having a good week. I'm excited to share that I've officially rebranded and launched the new website for my mobile bartending service: Full Proof Bartending [Link]. 

I always think back to how much fun your event was. If you know anyone in LA or OC looking for a craft bar setup for an upcoming party or wedding, I'd be honored if you kept me in mind. Also, if you happen to have any photos of the cocktails from your event, I'd love to see them! Hope all is well."

**To Wendy:**
"Hey Wendy, it's Joe! Hope you've been well. I'm reaching out because I just took a big step and launched the new website for my premium mobile bartending business, Full Proof Bartending [Link]. 

Working your event was a highlight, so I wanted to share the news with you. If you ever need a bartender in the future, or know anyone hosting an event who might want a full craft bar setup and clear ice, I'd love a referral. Let me know what you think of the site!"

---

## Notes for Joe:
- **Customization:** Feel free to tweak the first sentence to reference something specific about their event if it feels natural.
- **Timing:** Send these mid-morning or early afternoon on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday for the best response rate.
- **GBP Link:** If you prefer a Google review over a written testimonial for the website, swap the ask to: *"would you be open to leaving a quick review on my Google page? [Insert GBP Review Link]"*
