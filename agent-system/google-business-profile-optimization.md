# Google Business Profile Optimization: Full Proof Bartending

This document outlines the exact steps to optimize the Full Proof Bartending Google Business Profile (GBP) to align with our premium positioning, dominate local search visibility, and convert high-intent event inquiries. 

As a mobile service, our GBP is our digital storefront. It must communicate composed hospitality and premium execution immediately.

## 1. Core Business Information & Local SEO

**Business Name:** Full Proof Bartending
*Rule: Ensure this matches the website exactly. Do not keyword stuff the name (e.g., "Full Proof Bartending - Los Angeles") as it violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension.*

**Primary Category:** Bartending Service
**Secondary Categories:** 
- Mobile Caterer
- Event Service
- Wedding Service

**Service Areas (Max 20):** 
Since we are a mobile service without a public storefront, ensure your address is hidden (set as a Service Area Business). Add a mix of broad counties and specific high-wealth/high-intent cities:

## 2. Optimized Business Description

*This copy is designed to be elegant, readable, and conversion-oriented. It naturally weaves in high-value local search terms ("mobile bartending", "private events", "Los Angeles", "Orange County") while maintaining a warm, composed tone.*

**Description Copy (under 750 characters):**
"Full Proof Bartending provides premium mobile bartending for private events, weddings, and corporate gatherings across Los Angeles, Long Beach, and Orange County. We bring a fully self-contained, professional craft bar to your venue—complete with running water, LED lighting, and premium compostable drinkware. Our service is defined by warm, guest-first hospitality and exceptional attention to detail, including our signature hand-carved clear ice that elevates every cocktail. Whether you're hosting an intimate dinner or a large celebration, we handle the bar operations seamlessly so you can focus on your guests. Contact us to elevate your next event."

## 4. Photo & Media Strategy

Photos are the strongest proof point for the brand. Avoid stock photos. Upload high-quality, concrete visual evidence of the service. 

*SEO Hack: Rename photo files on your computer to describe the service and location (e.g., `los-angeles-mobile-bartender-clear-ice.jpg`) before uploading to Google.*

**Essential Photo Categories to Upload:**
1. **The Bar Setup:** Wide and detailed shots of the Tier B self-contained bar. Show the LED lighting, organized tools, running water/rinser, and clean aesthetic.
2. **The Ice (Visual Moat):** Close-ups of the hand-carved clear ice in the glass, and the ice press in action.
3. **The Drinks:** Beautifully garnished cocktails and clear ice details in clean, event-ready glassware or service cups.
4. **Action Shots:** You (Joe) behind the bar, demonstrating composed, attentive service and Southern hospitality.
5. **Event Context:** Wide shots showing the bar integrated into a beautiful event space or party.

## 5. Google Q&A Seeding

Google allows business owners to ask and answer their own questions. Seed these FAQs to handle objections and insert keywords.

**Q:** Do you provide the alcohol for the event?
**A:** As a dry hire mobile bartending service, our clients provide the alcohol. However, we provide comprehensive menu planning and an exact shopping list with quantities so your purchasing is straightforward and stress-free.

**Q:** What areas do you serve?
**A:** We are based in Long Beach, CA, and provide premium private event and wedding bartending across Los Angeles, Orange County, and the broader Southern California area.

**Q:** Can I book before choosing the cocktail menu?
**A:** Yes. For good-fit events, you can hold the date first and choose a rotating, custom, or decide-later cocktail path after. Base bartending starts at $599 for 3 hours or $699 for 4 hours, with Full Proof Pro Bar and Full Proof Elite Bar available as clear upgrades.

**Q:** Do premium upgrades require a bar rental?
**A:** Yes. Clear ice upgrades, smoke, espresso martinis, custom infusions, dehydrated citrus, premium garnish styling, and menu signage require Full Proof Pro Bar or Full Proof Elite Bar so presentation, workflow, ice handling, and guest experience stay controlled.
