# Local Growth Plan — Full Proof Bartending

**Markets:** Los Angeles, Long Beach, Orange County  
**Strategy:** Relationships first; broad ads second. **Sequence:** Long Beach (home base) → adjacent LA corridors → OC clusters that align with travel patterns.

This plan prioritizes **wedding and event planners**, **venues**, **caterers**, **private chefs**, **photographers**, and **event vendors** (with florists, rentals, and luxury picnic / dinner-party vendors as strong secondary targets).

---

## 1. Partner universe (who to list)

Build a living spreadsheet (see §6). For each category, aim for a mix of **volume** (steady inquiries) and **prestige** (portfolio and referrals).

| Tier | Partner type | Why they matter |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **A** | Planners (wedding + corporate + social) | Control vendor stacks; repeat referrals; gatekeepers for premium budgets. |
| **A** | Venues (permits, logistics, preferred lists) | Built-in lead flow; credibility when listed as preferred bar. |
| **A** | Caterers | Same guest footprint; bar + kitchen coordination reduces friction. |
| **B** | Private chefs | Intimate dinners and estates; high touch; recurring hosts. |
| **B** | Photographers | Visual proof of bar execution; cross-tags; vendor-room chemistry at weddings. |
| **B** | Event vendors (production, rentals, picnic/luxe picnic, experiential) | Same calendar; bundle-friendly; introductions to planners. |
| **C** | Florists, specialty rentals, DJs/bands | Secondary introductions; still valuable for networking loops and styled shoots. |

**Geographic clustering**

- **Long Beach:** Downtown / Bluff Park / Belmont Shore hosts; nearby planners who serve LB + LA clients.
- **LA:** Westside, Eastside pockets, Valley (where travel still pencils), DTLA venues and planners.
- **OC:** Newport / Laguna coastal events; Irvine / Costa Mesa corporate-adjacent planners; venues along PCH and inland estates.

**Qualifying signals (good-fit partners)**

- Premium positioning (copy, imagery, minimums, or editorial features).
- Active Instagram / portfolio with weddings or seated dinners.
- Clear inquiry path (not ghost-town sites).
- Evidence they care about guest experience, not just lowest vendor bid.

## 2. Outreach scripts

Tone: warm, professional, guest-first—aligned with Full Proof’s hospitality voice. Swap bracketed fields; keep messages short.

### 2.1 Instagram DM (cold or warm follow)

**Version A — planner / vendor**

> Hi [Name] — I’m Joe with **Full Proof Bartending**, luxury mobile bartending for LA, Long Beach & OC (clear ice, full craft setup, compostable glassware included).  
> I love how you approach [specific detail from their feed/site]. If you ever need a bar partner who shows up polished and coordinates cleanly with your team, I’d love to be on your short list.  
> Happy to send a one-pager + recent photos. Worth a quick chat?

**Version B — venue**

> Hi [Name], Joe here — **Full Proof Bartending**. We’re a premium mobile bar for private events and often work venues where outside bar service needs to look and operate like an extension of the space.  
> Is there a preferred vendor process or someone who handles beverage partner approvals? I’d love to introduce Full Proof properly.

### 2.2 Email (introduction)

**Subject:** Intro — Full Proof Bartending (mobile craft bar | LA · LB · OC)

> Hi [Name],  
>  
> I’m Joe Willeford, founder of **Full Proof Bartending** — luxury mobile bartending for weddings, private parties, and corporate gatherings across Los Angeles, Long Beach, and Orange County.  
>  
> We bring a self-contained craft bar plan, hand-carved clear ice, organized service tools, and an optional cups-and-garnishes package so the bar photographs beautifully and runs smoothly for your team and guests.  
>  
> I’m actively building relationships with planners and vendors I trust, and I’d love to learn how you like to coordinate bar service on [their specialty] events. If you’re open, I can share a short capability sheet and availability — or stop by for coffee if you’re local to [their area].  
>  
> Thank you for the work you put into guest experience.  
> Joe  
> [Website] | [Phone] | [Instagram]

### 2.3 Caterer / chef coordination angle

> Hi [Name] — Joe with Full Proof. When you’re running food programs for private events, I’d love to align bar pacing, dietary cues, and ice/service so guests never feel rushed between courses and cocktails.  
> Open to being a go-to bar partner for your clients who want something elevated beyond beer/wine?

### 2.4 Photographer collaboration angle

> Hi [Name] — your [shoot/wedding/reel] captured [specific compliment]. I’m building a short list of photographers who care about how the bar reads on-camera (glassware, ice, garnish discipline).  
> If you’re open to referrals both ways or a styled shoot down the line, I’d love to connect.

### 2.5 Follow-up bump (no reply after first touch)

> Hi [Name] — quick bump on my note below. No rush — if timing isn’t right, totally fine. If there’s a better contact for vendor partnerships, happy to reach out to them instead.

### 2.6 Post-event thank-you (after working together)

> [Name], thank you for trusting Full Proof on [event]. It was a pleasure supporting your team and guests.  
> If there’s anything I can do to make referring us easier (photos, one-liner for proposals, insurance/COI packet), say the word — and I’d love to stay in touch for the next one.

## 3. Follow-up cadence

Default rhythm after **first outbound message** (adjust if they engaged warmly — shorten gaps).

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **0** | First touch (DM or email). Personalize one concrete detail. |
| **3–5** | Single bump if no reply (different channel if appropriate: email ↔ IG). |
| **14** | Value touch: share relevant reel/post, styled bar photo, or “thought of you” intro to mutual vendor — no hard pitch. |
| **30** | Soft check-in; ask if they prefer quarterly updates or event-date-driven outreach only. |
| **90** | Seasonal note (planning cycles, NYE, gala season) + single clear CTA (“15-min intro?”). |

**Rules**

- **Cap** at four unsolicited pings before pausing unless they engage; move them to “nurture” (see §6).
- If they say “later,” **calendar** the next touch for their suggested window.
- After any successful collaboration: **48-hour** thank-you + **30-day** referral toolkit offer.

## 4. Referral ideas

- **Planner incentive (non-cash):** Priority hold on peak Saturdays after X referrals; co-branded “vendor spotlight” post (their choice of asset).
- **Venue loop:** Offer to leave printed cards or a minimal tabletop card only where venues allow; emphasize liability-friendly, insured, quiet setup.
- **Styled shoot trades:** Partner with photographer + florist + planner for one portfolio refresh per quarter (LB-first).
- **Caterer bundles:** Pre-written language they can paste into proposals (“premium mobile bar option”).
- **Chef dinners:** Tasting menus + paired low-ABV or classic builds; chefs recommend for NYE / milestones.
- **Client alumni:** Use existing client outreach patterns (`client-outreach-texts.md`) for photo/testimonial/referral loops.

---

## 5. Local SEO ideas (supporting layer)

- **Google Business Profile:** Keep categories tight; service-area radius honest; collect reviews that mention city + event type (see `google-business-profile-optimization.md`).
- **Location pages / copy:** Natural mentions of LA, Long Beach, OC neighborhoods on site (no doorway spam); align with real travel policy.
- **Vendor backlinks:** Venues and planners linking “preferred vendors” — prioritize **earned** listings over paid farms.
- **Neighborhood proof:** Geo-tagged reels/stills from recognizable regional venues (permission-aware).

## 6. Simple tracking fields

Use a spreadsheet or Notion database; one row per **organization** (not per individual), with linked contacts.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|--------|
| **Partner ID** | Short code (e.g. `PLN-LB-001`). |
| **Organization name** | Legal or DBA. |
| **Category** | Planner / Venue / Caterer / Chef / Photographer / Event vendor / Other. |
| **Market** | LB / LA / OC / Multi. |
| **Priority** | A / B / C. |
| **Primary contact** | Name + role. |
| **Email / Phone / IG** | Best paths. |
| **Source** | IG / referral / event / search / other. |
| **Stage** | Research → Contacted → Engaged → Pilot event → Active partner → Nurture → Closed. |
| **Last touch date** | Auto-sort for follow-ups. |
| **Next action** | One concrete task + owner. |
| **Next action date** | Due date. |
| **Warmth** | Cold / warm intro / worked together. |
| **Notes** | Personalization hooks, preferences, kids’ names only if appropriate — keep professional. |
| **Referrals sent (count)** | Simple tally YTD. |
| **Events together (count)** | Trust metric. |

**Weekly habit (30 minutes):** Filter `Next action date` ≤ today; send five touches; update stages.
