# Full Proof Visual Asset Direction

Updated: 2026-05-14

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not use old bar photos.
- Do not reuse the same image on the homepage unless there is a deliberate conversion reason. Repeated assets belong on different pages, not multiple homepage sections.
- Do not use photos with visible Two for the Road branding.
- Do not use photos with cars, parking lots, garages, dumpsters, messy home prep, cluttered counters, or weak casual backgrounds.
- Do not use photos that make Full Proof feel like a hobby setup, even if the drink or bar equipment is good.
- Do not use AI-looking rooftop/event scenes or fake brand environments.

## What The Site Should Use First

Prioritize visual assets in this order:

1. Clear ice closeups with dramatic light, clean background, or branded/stamped detail.
2. Finished cocktails with clear ice and intentional garnish.
3. Joe in action only when the background does not cheapen the brand.
4. Detail shots: hands, tools, garnish, ice carving, smoke, citrus, premium bottles.
5. New polished bar photos only after they are captured or provided.

## Photo Editing Direction

For strong images with imperfect lighting or background:

- Crop tight enough to remove room clutter, cars, parking lots, lamps, wall outlets, plastic bins, or casual household details.
- Darken or blur background when possible.
- Increase contrast slightly.
- Keep ice highlights crisp.
- Preserve natural cocktail color; do not over-saturate.
- Favor vertical crops for hero/about and square crops for gallery/detail tiles.

## Needed Shot Slots

When polished photos/videos are provided, replace current placeholders with:

- Hero: cinematic clear ice or cocktail scene with negative space for headline.
- Booking section: finished cocktail or clean ice detail, not a bar-wide setup.
- Services: one image each for wedding/private/brand if available.
- Packages: clean package proof such as clear ice, cocktail pair, garnish, menu card.
- About: Joe looking composed and professional behind service, with clean background.
- Google Business Profile: logo, hero cocktail, clear ice closeup, Joe/service action, detail collage.

## Video Direction

Use short clips for social and future site motion:

- Ice stamp reveal.
- Clear cube in glass.
- Orange twist/flame.
- Cocktail smoke or smoke bubble only where it looks refined.
- Clean pour over clear ice.

Avoid clips with messy rooms, old bar branding, clutter, or shaky unfinished setup context.
