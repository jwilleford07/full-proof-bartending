# Competitor Pricing Audit

Updated: 2026-05-14

## Market Read

Full Proof should not price against bare hourly bartender services. The better competitive set is polished mobile bartending and mobile bar service for weddings, private events, corporate events, brand activations, and high-design gatherings across Los Angeles, Long Beach, and Orange County.

Visible market anchors reviewed:

- Social Pour: mobile bar packages starting around $950, $1,250, and $2,000, with broader LA mobile-bar guidance around $1,200-$4,500+.
- Thumbs Up Cocktails: $1,500 beer/wine service, $2,400 full bar with cocktails, $3,200 custom cocktail package.
- LAX Bartending: $650 essential, $1,250 signature, $2,400 luxury.
- Curly Bartender: typical 50-guest BYOB signature investment around $750-$1,350 for 4 hours.
- Free Spirits Mobile Bar: $430-$470/hour with a 3-hour minimum, before gratuity/travel.
- Mixed Bar Services: $50/hour per bartender plus $250 portable bar rental, lower-end service-only positioning.

## Pricing Decision

The former public anchor of `$599 / 3 hours` and `$699 / 4 hours` was approachable but under-leveraged given Full Proof's differentiators:

- 20 years in hospitality.
- 11+ years behind the bar.
- Private-event bartending experience dating back to 2017.
- Real clear ice production background.
- Hand-carved clear ice included for Full Proof-served cocktails.
- Premium drinkware and planning guidance.
- Clear shopping guidance for client-provided alcohol.

## Homepage Pricing Ladder

Use this structure publicly:

- **Base Bartending:** `$599 / 3 hours` or `$699 / 4 hours`.
- **GoBar Pro:** `+$100`, recommended for up to `40` guests.
- **GoBar Elite:** `+$200`, required for `41-125` guests and optional for smaller events that want a larger luxury footprint.
- **126+ guests:** manual review before instant booking.
- **Premium add-ons:** require GoBar Pro or Elite so presentation, workflow, ice handling, and guest experience stay controlled.

## Deposit Language

Use date-hold language without wiring a payment link until Stripe or Square is ready:

> Hold the date now. Finalize the menu later.

> If you already know you need polished bar service, ask about reserving standard service with a date-hold deposit. Joe will confirm availability and finalize scope, menu direction, and shopping guidance after the date is secured.

## Guardrails

- Keep the entry point approachable with transparent base rates, not discount language.
- Do not use `cheap`, `budget`, `discount`, or urgency gimmicks.
- Do not make the whole brand feel on sale.
- Do not bury high-touch weddings or brand events inside the standard price.
- Keep specialty ice, additional bartenders, extended service, custom menus, and deeper planning as quoted upgrades.
