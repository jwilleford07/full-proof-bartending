Bar tiers

Two rental levels, depending on how much bar you want carried for you.

Full Proof Pro Bar is a $100 compact premium footprint for events up to 40 guests. Full Proof Elite Bar is a $200 larger luxury footprint required for 41-125 guests and optional for smaller events that want the bar to anchor the room.

Full Proof Pro Bar | +$100

Compact, clean, and often the smartest fit.

Best for smaller get-togethers, rooftop load-ins, apartment courtyards, tighter patios, and events up to 40 guests where logistics matter as much as presentation. It keeps the service polished while staying practical.

The more impressive visual upgrade.

A larger, warmer, more substantial dark-wood bar profile for 41-125 guests, weddings, brand events, private estates, and parties where the bar should look like part of the event design. This is the one to choose when presentation and photos matter.
