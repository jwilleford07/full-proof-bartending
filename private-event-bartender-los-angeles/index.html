Before booking, confirm what wrap-down includes so leftover ingredients, bar tools, ice, trash, and the service area do not quietly become the host's problem.

Questions to ask

Use these before signing with any bartender.

CoverageHow many bartenders do they recommend for your guest count, service window, and drink style? Who arrives for setup, and how early?
SuppliesWho handles cups, garnishes, ice, mixers, tools, batching, nonalcoholic options, and shopping guidance?
Venue fitCan they work without a built-in bar, sink, or house power if the event space does not provide those things?
ProfessionalismCan they explain insurance needs, compliant dry-hire expectations, venue coordination, and day-of communication clearly?
