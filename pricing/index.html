Package paths

Pick by event shape, not by mystery package names.

Base service is for simple events with an existing counter, table, or venue bar. Pro is the compact finished bar. Elite is the statement event bar and the required path for 41-125 guests.

If the event is larger than 125 guests, has unusual load-in, needs deep staffing, or calls for heavier production, it goes through manual review before any payment step.

Responsible dry-hire model

You buy the alcohol. Full Proof helps you avoid the scramble.

Full Proof operates as a dry-hire service. The host purchases alcohol legally, and Full Proof guides quantities, menu fit, ice needs, mixers, garnish basics, setup, service, and cleanup.

RBS-certified bartender. ID checks when needed. Service may be refused or paused for underage guests, visibly intoxicated guests, unsafe conditions, or situations that put guests or the bar at risk.
