Terms

Clear expectations make better events.

These terms summarize the basic rules for Full Proof Bartending inquiries, date holds, host responsibilities, and operational SMS communication.

Date holds

A date is held after confirmation and payment.

Instant-book eligible events may continue to secure checkout for a 50% date-hold deposit. The event date is not held until availability is confirmed and the required deposit or agreed payment is completed.

Alcohol

Host-purchased alcohol.

Full Proof operates as dry-hire bartending. The host is responsible for purchasing alcohol legally unless a separate compliant arrangement is made. Full Proof can provide shopping guidance and quantities.

Safety

Responsible adult service.

Guests who appear under 21 may be ID checked. Service may be refused or paused for underage guests, visibly intoxicated guests, unsafe behavior, unclear alcohol responsibility, or unsafe venue conditions.

Event scope

Quotes depend on real logistics.

Pricing and availability may depend on event date, city, venue, guest count, service hours, load-in, parking, power, water, bar footprint, staffing needs, insurance requirements, and selected service upgrades.

Events with 126+ guests, complex venues, unclear access, custom activations, or expanded staffing may require manual review before a quote or date-hold path is confirmed.

SMS terms

Full Proof event inquiry and booking SMS.

By checking the SMS consent box or otherwise opting in, you authorize Full Proof Bartending to send text messages related to your inquiry, booking, quote, date-hold, event logistics, and post-event follow-up. Consent is not required to purchase services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can cancel SMS at any time by texting STOP. After STOP, Full Proof Bartending will confirm unsubscribe status and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you opt in again. Text HELP for assistance or email joe@fullproofbartending.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See the Privacy Policy for privacy details.
