# Full Proof Photo QA

## Core Positioning

Full Proof Bartending should read as premium mobile bartending first, with clear ice as a major differentiator and trust signal.

Lead with:

- 20 years in hospitality.
- 11+ years behind the bar.
- Private event bartending experience dating back to 2017 through restaurant-led events and private service.
- A clear cocktail ice company built from zero knowledge into a supplier for 10 bars and restaurants around LA County.
- Distiller Magazine credibility: Joe was quoted in Camper English's article on large-format clear ice production.

Do not imply bar service is new. This version of the brand is new; the hospitality and bartending experience is not.

## Photo Approval Standard

Use photos that clearly support at least one of these:

- Polished mobile bartending service.
- Clean professional bar setup.
- Finished cocktails.
- Hand-carved clear ice, branded ice, ice columns, or ice production credibility.
- Joe as a composed hospitality operator.

Reject or delete photos with:

- Old "Two for the Road" branding.
- Parking lots, cars, dumpsters, garages, or casual loading zones.
- Folding-table energy, backyard clutter, home-bar clutter, or messy prep scenes.
- Weak standalone details that do not immediately communicate premium service or clear ice craft.
- Obvious AI artifacts, stock-photo energy, fake brand marks, or unrealistic props.
- Bad flash, awkward indoor test shots, or photos that make the service feel improvised.

## Clear Ice Rule

Do not treat clear ice images as unrelated to bartending. Clear ice is a key proof point because Joe built a real clear cocktail ice operation serving LA County bars and restaurants.

When choosing between weak bar photos and strong clear-ice photos, prefer the strong clear-ice asset and strengthen the copy around hospitality and service.

## Copy Guardrails

Avoid:

- "New to bartending."
- "Just starting out."
- Over-indexing on clear ice so the brand reads like an ice company instead of a bartending service.
- Publicly naming private or celebrity clients unless Joe confirms permission.

Prefer:

- "20 years in hospitality."
- "11+ years behind the bar."
- "Private event bartending experience dating back to 2017."
- "Clear ice backed by real LA production experience."
- "Built a clear cocktail ice company from zero into a supplier for bars and restaurants across LA County."

## Workflow

1. Inspect the image itself before using or deleting it.
2. Check where the image is referenced before deletion.
3. Replace weak live-site images with stronger approved assets before deleting files.
4. Update internal handoff docs if asset lists change.
5. Preserve clear-ice credibility and Distiller Magazine proof wherever relevant.
